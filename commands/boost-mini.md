description: Grammar-only boost - fixes wording and sentence structure, adds nothing
---

`/boost-mini <request>` is rewritten by the boost hook before it ever reaches
you: grammar and sentence structure only, nothing added, no gaps filled. It
forces that mode for the one prompt without touching the settings `/boost`
saved.

If you are reading this, the hook stayed silent - the request was too short, or
the rewriter was unavailable. Carry out the request as it was typed.
